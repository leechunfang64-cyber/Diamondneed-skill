## Keyword Types To Collect

Prioritize precise intent keywords:

- tool keywords: generator, converter, downloader, editor, automation
- solution keywords: how to, best way to, alternative, template
- purchase keywords: price, paid, subscription, download, buy
- comparison keywords: X vs Y, best X, alternative to X
- scenario keywords: for teachers, for sellers, for students, for short videos, for Xiaohongshu

Distinguish broad traffic from precise traffic:

- Broad keyword example: `free ai tools`
- Precise keyword example: `ai image to video generator`

Precise traffic is usually more valuable for conversion than broad traffic.
