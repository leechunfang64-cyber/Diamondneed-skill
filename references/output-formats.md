## 4. Decision Language

Use direct language:

- `Go Now`: evidence is strong enough to build a focused MVP now.
- `Small Bet`: evidence is promising but not enough for full build; test cheaply first.
- `Observe`: keep watching because key signals are missing.
- `Reject`: do not spend serious time because the business logic is weak or risky.

Avoid vague conclusions such as:

```text
可以考虑试试。
市场有潜力。
后续持续关注。
```
